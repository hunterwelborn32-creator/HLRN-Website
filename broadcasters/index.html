HLRN Channel FAQ

Broadcast information

What is the HLRN Channel?

The HLRN Channel is the broadcast home for High Line Racing Network league coverage, featuring Sunday Night League coverage from Tommy Rogers and Monday Night League coverage from Randy Schweitzer and RSI Broadcasting.

When does HLRN race?

Sunday Night League and Monday Night League broadcasts are scheduled for 8:30 PM Eastern each week.

Where can I watch?

Sunday broadcasts are available through the High Line Racing YouTube channel. Monday broadcasts are available through RSI Broadcasting on YouTube. Use the watch buttons above to open either channel.
