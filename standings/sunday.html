Long-Form Race Breakdown

Trevor Haley started from the pole, but Ethan Fonseca Moreno took the lead on lap 1. Moreno controlled much of the opening portion before Juan Escamilla moved to the point on lap 19. Moreno reclaimed the lead on lap 22.

The first caution came on lap 5 for a slow car involving #8 Jared Philpott. Philpott recovered from that early trouble and from a 30th-place starting position to eventually win the race.

The second caution came on lap 27 involving #10. Race control issued repairs-needed calls to Larkin Boyer and Brock Piper during that caution cycle, along with additional admin and Lucky Dog instructions.

Haley returned to the lead on lap 38 before another caution involving the #12. Ethan Eckert, Juan Escamilla, Nicholas Baumann, Jared Philpott and Dylan C Jones all spent time at the front through the middle portion of the race.

The fifth caution came on lap 59 involving #012 Ethan Eckert. The sixth and final official caution began on lap 65 for a slow car involving #86. From there, Michigan settled into a long green-flag run to the finish.

The supplied session-event history records Jared Philpott taking the lead from Dylan C Jones on lap 115. Official scoring at the finish put Philpott P1, Dylan C Jones P2, Trevor Haley P3, Joshua McKinney P4 and Matthew Brown P5.

Jared Philpott: P30 → P1. Dylan C Jones: P32 → P2. Two massive drives through the field decided the Michigan podium.

The official race summary reports 18 lead changes. The session-event list supplied for this report contains 19 lead-change entries, so the website keeps the official summary number while still displaying every supplied lead-change event.
