Headline of the Night

Row 11 became the fight for the win.

Ethan Eckert started 21st and Kenny Reel started 22nd. They finished first and second, separated by only 0.048 seconds. Together, the top two gained 40 positions.

What Defined the Race

The race began with pole sitter Kenwood Ramsey leading the first 17 laps, then shifted through Donny Beach and Ryan Wilson21 before Eckert took over on lap 38. Two later caution cycles briefly gave Carson Freeman the lead, but Eckert reclaimed it both times.

The six cautions repeatedly reset the field, and 47 penalty calls were recorded in the session event log, making pit-road execution and surviving restarts major parts of the outcome.

Winner Spotlight

#012 Ethan Eckert

Monday Night Winner

Ethan Eckert

Eckert started 21st in a 29-car field and needed only 38 laps to reach the lead. Once he got there, the #012 became the central car in the race. He led 86 laps, more than every other leader combined, and controlled 68.8% of the entire event.

His night was not simply a green-flag runaway. Cautions at laps 63 and 85 shuffled the order and briefly handed the lead to Carson Freeman. On each occasion Eckert took it back one lap later. The final caution at lap 110 gave the field one more shot, and Kenny Reel closed to within 0.048 seconds at the finish.

Race Story

The race broken into phases

Laps 1–17 • Opening Run

Ramsey controls the start

Kenwood Ramsey launched from the pole and led the opening 17 laps. The first caution came at lap 6 involving the #82, and the second followed at lap 11 involving the #91. Those early yellows prevented the race from settling into a long opening green-flag rhythm.

Laps 18–37 • New Leaders Emerge

Beach and Wilson trade the front

Donny Beach took the lead on lap 18. Ryan Wilson21 moved ahead on lap 29, Beach reclaimed the point on lap 30, and Wilson returned to the lead on lap 34 during the third caution sequence. The front of the field had changed rapidly before the race reached one-third distance.

Laps 38–64 • The #012 Arrives

Eckert takes command

Ethan Eckert took the lead on lap 38 after starting 21st. From that point forward, the race largely ran through him. The #012 controlled the longest sustained stretch at the front and began building what would become an 86-lap race-leading total.

Laps 63–88 • Strategy Interrupts the Leader

Freeman gets two brief chances

A caution on lap 63 involving the #87 changed pit-cycle positioning. Carson Freeman moved to the lead on lap 65, but Eckert retook it on lap 66. The same sequence repeated after the lap-85 caution: Freeman led on lap 87, Eckert reclaimed the point on lap 88.

Laps 89–109 • Long Green Stretch

Eckert rebuilds control

Once back in front, Eckert stayed there through a substantial green-flag stretch. Behind him, drivers such as Kenny Reel, Nicholas Moody, Hunter Welborn, Brian Hebbard2, and others continued fighting for position as the top 10 compressed around the leader.

Laps 110–125 • Final Sprint

Reel makes it a thriller

The final caution came at lap 110 involving the #03. The race resumed with a short run to the finish. Kenny Reel, who had started 22nd, emerged as Eckert's biggest threat and closed the final margin to 0.048 seconds. Moody, Thompson, and Welborn completed the top five.

Best Comeback

Eckert and Reel each gained 20 spots. Ricky Miles gained 17, while Hunter Welborn gained 14.

Biggest Early Loss

Front-row starter Chris James completed seven laps and was scored 27th, a drop of 25 positions from his second-place start.

Survival Story

Ricky Miles started 28th, completed all 125 laps, recorded zero incidents, and finished 11th — one spot outside the top 10.

Why the Cautions Mattered

Only 18 of the 125 laps were officially caution laps, but the yellows repeatedly changed strategy, track position, and pit-road timing. The lap-33 caution was especially busy, producing multiple repairs, pit-road calls, admin actions, and closed-pit issues before racing resumed.

Later cautions at laps 63 and 85 interrupted long stretches of Eckert control and temporarily put Carson Freeman at the front. The final yellow at lap 110 created the closing sprint that allowed Kenny Reel to challenge for the win.

Race-Control Snapshot

The session log recorded 47 penalty calls. The largest categories were unsafe pit entrance (12), repairs needed (10), passing under yellow (8), Lucky Dog (5), entering closed pits (5), admin caution (4), and excessive pit speed (3).

Many calls were later cleared by the system or an admin. The report lists the recorded activity but does not assign fault beyond the session log wording.
