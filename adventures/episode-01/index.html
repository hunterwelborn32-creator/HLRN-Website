8 AM EST · CHAPTER 01

Lark & Nark

The Morning Begins

@Lark is a driver from Georgia with a pet seal named Nark. Tonight is a huge race at his home track, and with all eyes on him, he knows he needs to stay completely focused. But letting go of the past isn’t always easy. It looks like there’s someone he just can’t stop thinking about. Can Lark put those thoughts behind him and lock in before the green flag? And what exactly are his race day routines that prepare him for the biggest nights of the season? Tune in next hour to follow Larkin’s journey and catch up with the rest of the drivers as race day at EchoPark Speedway continues.

9 AM EST · CHAPTER 02

The Adventures Begin

The Phone Goes Missing

@Lark and Nark kick off race day with their usual routine. First, it’s time to get dressed, then sit down for a hearty bowl of Lug Nut Cereal before scrolling through the morning news. That’s when Lark spots what could be a major update on @Juan Escamilla status for tonight’s race.

With the news digested, the pair carry on with their race day routine the only way they know how, an all-out dance party to the Backstreet Boys. But wait… isn’t that Jerry’s blind squirrel?

In the middle of the dance off, the squirrel suddenly snatches Lark’s phone and bolts out the camper window. Where is he headed, and what’s so important about that phone?

Meanwhile, completely unaware of the squirrel’s latest theft, Race Control’s @#71 Jerry Fassett is enjoying a peaceful morning, sipping a refreshing Pedley’s Blinker Fluid while staring at his computer. Suddenly, he catches a strange smell… then smoke begins pouring from beneath his motorized chair.

The engine has officially given up. Now Jerry is in full panic mode as he tries to nurse the smoking chair back to the garage for emergency repairs. But who will be there to help him? Stay tuned throughout the day as The Adventures of High Line continues.

10 AM EST · CHAPTER 03

Paddock Pursuit

The Hunt Is On

@Lark is a complete mess. He has to get his phone back, and fast. He tells Nark there’s secret content on it that absolutely cannot end up on Blind Squirrel Media. With no time to waste, Lark throws on his football pads… completely forgetting that he’s racing tonight, not playing football.

Now the hunt is on. But where will he begin searching for the squirrel?

Meanwhile, @Bryce Hinton and @Alex Leebaw are starting their own race day routines. Bryce is relaxing outside his hauler, sipping on a first-class martini while reading from his ever-growing encyclopedia. Across the way, Alex is getting himself race ready in his own unique fashion, building up his energy for the night ahead.

Will Lark recover his phone before its secrets are exposed? Stay tuned as The Adventures of High Line continues.

11 AM EST · CHAPTER 04

Secrets & Sabotage

The Squirrel Strikes Again

@Lark finally tracks down the Blind Squirrel, only to discover the furry thief had just cleaned @#31 Scott Wise out at his own rigged poker game. Scott is so upset, he withdraws from the race. The moment Lark locks eyes with him, the squirrel freezes in panic, clutches the phone even tighter, kicks over his chair, and bolts as fast as his titanium leg will carry him.

Meanwhile, Nark strolls over to the Helix Motorsports hauler, Sony speakers blasting at full volume. The sound is like nails on a chalkboard to @Bryce Hinton who immediately covers his ears in agony. Nark finally reveals the truth about who sabotaged Bryce’s JBL speakers.

@#100 Eric Hayden!!

Bryce slowly lowers his hands, his expression turning from irritation to disbelief… and then to pure determination. Eric has just become his next target.

POST-RACE FINALE · CHAPTER 15

EchoPark Finale

Victory Lane & The Cliffhanger

Congratulations to @craig8392 and Prime X on an outstanding victory! The team celebrates in Victory Lane as Nark the Seal happily joins in on the festivities.

Meanwhile, tonight’s race produced no shortage of drama. @Juan Escamilla never could shake that infamous Taco Bell parasite, making a shocking left turn through the field before heading straight back to the nearest port-a-john.

As if that wasn’t enough, the night’s wreck-filled action apparently caught the attention of Georgia SWAT. By the end of the race, nearly half the field had been rounded up and hauled off to the drunk tank to sober up after the chaos that unfolded on track.

Finally Nark finally catches up with @Jim Segredo and spills everything about @Bryce Hinton. He also reveals the secret hiding on Lark’s phone. Now Jim is armed with a lot of information, but what will he do with it during the two-week break before the drivers head to Charlotte? Find out on the next episode, August 9th.
